## Direction contract

THESIS: Read the lap the way a tyre reads a track, through grip. The ground is vulcanised rubber, and the only colours are the compound bands. Refuses the broadcast look of team-colour glass panels and chyrons on blue-black.

OWN-WORLD: matte rubber black ground with fine concentric sidewall grooves; raised-rubber lettering (extended grotesk caps, embossed by one raking light) set on arcs; compound bands as the only hues: soft red = model's pick, medium yellow = your pick, hard white = data, inter green = correct, wet blue = braking. Team colour appears only as a thin chip beside a driver code.

STORY: Visitors learn the model reads style, not speed alone; believe it because every guess cites a corner; they guess, lose or win, and play again.

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed 3D asphalt track under raking light, the car laying a compound-coloured rubber line. A giant sidewall arc bleeds off the bottom-left: corner ticks round its rim, lap progress as the band, speed and gear set inside. Right edge: the 20-driver line-up as embossed rows whose bars re-sort live; clicking a row is your guess. Evidence line along the bottom.

FORM: candidate 7 of 7 (tyre sidewall and compound bands).
