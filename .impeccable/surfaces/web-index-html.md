Scope: the whole BLIND LAP web app (home hub, play, reveal, duel, teammates, daily, Driver DNA, Telemetry lab, How it knows). Visitor mode: Experience.
Audience: F1 fans playing a round; portfolio visitors judging depth. Job: pick a mode quickly, watch and hear an anonymous lap, guess, see why each model guessed what it did.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A race poster you can play. Light paper, heavy black type, F1 red as the one brand block, team colours as big flat fields; the 3D circuit is a framed picture on the poster. Refuses the dark broadcast cockpit and the previous rubber-black world.

OWN-WORLD: paper #F1F0EC ground, ink #111 type and 3px rules, F1 red #E10600 for the brand block and primary action, team colour only as large flat fields or chips that belong to a named driver; sharp corners, no shadows, no glass; Archivo extended black for display, normal width for text, tabular numerals.

STORY: Visitors land on a hub that says what this is in one line and offers four ways to play; in a lap they always know where they are and how to get back; the reveal tells them who it was and how each model did.

FIRST VIEWPORT: home: left 40% a red block with huge white "Who's driving?" and a one-line lede, right a framed 3D view orbiting a circuit; below, four mode tiles in one row. Play: top bar with Menu back and the session, framed 3D view filling the left, ranking column right, telemetry band under the view with huge speed numerals.

FORM: owner-pinned direction C (bold poster) from the mockup round; replaces the tyre-sidewall world. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
